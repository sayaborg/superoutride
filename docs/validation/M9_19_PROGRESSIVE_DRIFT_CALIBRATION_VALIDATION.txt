LIMITS
Handling remains DEV_UNCALIBRATED. This is one ordinary-input path with sampled
margins, not a universal equilibrium map or completed human touch/keyboard feel.
The 40-second shallow preparation is diagnostic, not a required player technique
or a claim of an exact steady grip circle. Entry overshoots near 21.4 degrees.
Every speed/gear/state, +/-5-degree state disturbances, all-nine-vehicle drifting,
forward drift flight on actual courses and lower-G high-speed cornering remain
unproven. Cumulative circular trajectory distance is not useful forward flight.
Strong-brake rear lock/spin, repeated wheelie/stoppie overturn, ABS/TCS and large-yaw
raster perception remain separate work. No live human browser control was certified.
