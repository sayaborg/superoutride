Resolved architecture:
- travel-direction feedback is the exact unit-coefficient body-to-CG-travel coordinate
  transformation, not an assist-strength value;
- the retired travelDirectionGain state, setter, key table, touch selector, HUD row and
  compatibility path are absent;
- one Driver-owned yawRateBaseline is the sole new persistent dynamic value and is not a second
  physical yaw, target yaw, force cache or HUD value;
- the baseline uses exact exponential low-pass evolution and exposes only the zero-DC transient
  yaw remainder to steering;
- automatic steering is betaTravel minus the yaw transient term, clamped by A before driver offset
  is added and before the final mechanical M clamp;
- A is derived at profile compilation as maxRoadWheelSteer minus steeringOffsetMax and is never a
  separately authored or selectable value;
- the washout state advances through saturation and absent contact, while construction and
  recovery synchronize it to current physical yaw rate;
- steering-only low-speed regularization is distinct from tire-slip regularization;
- one common mechanics path serves CAR and BIKE without reading tire force, slip, utilization,
  acceleration telemetry, Guide, camera, route or screen state;
- symmetric apply/release steering response is validated both at profile compilation and vehicle
  construction;
- ordinary player and rival construction share compiled-profile defaults without sharing mutable
  calibration or filter state;
- profile switching and recovery preserve the three selected values, clone the nested actuator
  response and reset only the private washout baseline;
- no renderer, camera, route, topology, race-progress, SurfaceMap or tire-law invariant changes.
