Resolved architecture:
- M5CameraRig owns exactly one finite BODY_FIXED / MOVEMENT_FOLLOW mode;
- BODY_FIXED is the default and assigns camera yaw exactly from authoritative vehicle body yaw on
  every camera update;
- MOVEMENT_FOLLOW retains the existing M8.2 body-pitch-plane world-velocity derivation and
  low-speed last-valid-direction hold;
- retained movement yaw is derived presentation memory only and is never consumed by vehicle
  physics, route, race or topology;
- reset clears derived yaw/vertical history without changing the selected mode;
- P is the sole keyboard camera-mode binding and all three browser composition roots use it;
- mobile BODY/MOVE buttons derive from the same two camera modes and mutate the same rig field;
- BODY_FIXED draws TRAVEL YAW from actual derived movement direction relative to the body camera;
- MOVEMENT_FOLLOW draws BODY YAW relative to the movement camera;
- no Guide correction, yaw damping, safety camera, vehicle-specific branch or hidden view recovery
  is introduced;
- chainage depth, D_cam, player centering, body-pitch follow, zero roll and sprite authority remain
  unchanged.
