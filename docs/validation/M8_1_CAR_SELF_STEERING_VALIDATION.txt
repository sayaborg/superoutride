  clears immediately when the digital button is released;

  the driver offset;

- neutral input aims along predicted CG travel direction and produces early countersteer when body
  sideslip or yaw rate requires it;
- the 31-degree mechanical road-wheel stop remains the only hard steering-angle limit;
- 15:1 steering ratio derives the displayed handwheel angle, including +/-465 degrees at lock;
- touch steering uses independent left/right digital buttons with multi-pointer neutral handling;
- the CAR HUD shows L/N/R input, a rotated steering-wheel marker, handwheel angle, road-wheel angle,
