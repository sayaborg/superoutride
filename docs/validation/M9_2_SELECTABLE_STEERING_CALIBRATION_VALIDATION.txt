Resolved architecture:
- the common target remains travelDirectionGain*betaTravel - yawRate*yawPreviewTime + driverOffset;
- travel-direction gain scales only betaTravel and the yaw-preview coefficient remains independent;
- one ArcadeVehicleState.steeringCalibration owns the three current calibration values;
- browser-player defaults are gain 0.5, yaw preview 0.12 s and symmetric 0.375 s traversal;
- gain choices are exactly 0.3/0.4/0.5/0.6/0.7 through keys/numpad 4 through 8;
- yaw-preview choices are exactly 0/0.06/0.09/0.12/0.15/0.18 s and Y cycles them;
- symmetric steering traversal choices are exactly 0.25/0.375/0.5/0.625 s and T cycles them;
- every selectable steering-actuator response has applyRate equal to releaseRate;
- touch exposes all exact choices and the HUD reads the vehicle-instance state without owning a
  duplicate active value;
- manual/automatic recovery preserves calibration and safe-spawn profile reconstruction passes it
  as one unit;
- ordinary rivals and general mechanics construction retain compiled profile defaults;
- the selected actuator response overrides only the ordinary steering actuator channel; throttle
  and brake continue to consume their compiled profile rates;
- input arbitration, tire/contact/wheel mechanics, final road-wheel steeringResponseTau, camera,
  renderer, route and topology remain unchanged.
