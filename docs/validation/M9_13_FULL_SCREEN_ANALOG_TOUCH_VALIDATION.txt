SUPER OUTRIDE — M9.13 FULL-SCREEN ANALOG TOUCH VALIDATION

Scope

M9.13 replaces the legacy fixed touch driving panels with one full-screen relative analog touch surface while preserving the existing vehicle-state and mechanics authorities.

Validated behavior:
- pointer-down in the left viewport half owns STEERING for that pointer;
- pointer-down in the right viewport half owns ACCEL/BRAKE for that pointer;
- pointer-down position is the neutral origin and the role stays fixed after crossing the center line;
- steering uses normalized horizontal displacement in [-1,+1];
- upward pedal displacement publishes throttle [0,1] and downward displacement publishes brake [0,1];
- one steering pointer and one pedal pointer may operate simultaneously;
- held analog displacement uses DIRECT apply semantics against the existing actuator amount;
- release/cancel removes DIRECT ownership and the existing actuator release rate returns the channel toward neutral;
- keyboard input remains digital and rate-limited;
- full-scale slide distance is 25% of viewport short side, clamped to 72–120 CSS px;
- steering-wheel/pedal origin indicators, direction vector and percentage are presentation-only;
- legacy fixed driving panels remain hidden in touch-capable layouts while selector controls remain available;
- no touch-specific vehicle state, rack, pedal state, tire state, vehicle/course branch, steering law, handling law, camera authority, route authority or renderer authority is added.
