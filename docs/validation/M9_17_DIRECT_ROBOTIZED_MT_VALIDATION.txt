4. Implemented causal boundary

- The forward robotized MT derives RPM from authoritative driven-wheel Omega and
  the selected total gear ratio. Gear is its only dynamic memory; ENG is calibration.
- RPM and torque fields are output caches, not inputs to the next drive calculation.
- Remove launch slip, independent RPM response, shift duration, shift timer,
  shift direction and timed drive cutoff. No clutch/converter substitute remains.
- Shift at most one adjacent gear per substep; recompute torque immediately using
  that gear. No drive blanking, interpolation, cooldown or wheelspin-specific branch.
- Compile separate up/down thresholds and require downshift < upshift*next/current
  for each adjacent ratio. Current nine-profile thresholds and ratios satisfy this
  stronger hysteresis condition and are retained without arbitrary retuning.
- All nine profiles retain their positive torque knots; only synthetic zero-torque
  redline endpoints and the three retired coupling/timing parameters are removed.
- One C1 averaged fuel-cut factor is full through upshift RPM and zero at redline.
  The positive engine curve itself does not become zero at redline. There is no
  duplicate linear redline factor and no clamp on wheel speed or observed RPM.
- At rest RPM is zero, while curve lookup uses an explicit idle torque floor for
  no-stall launch. Zero throttle delivers zero drive. Engine inertia, active engine
  braking and overrev damage are outside this reduced power-delivery model.
- ENG 1/1.5/2/3/4, K/touch/HUD and recovery/replacement retention remain unchanged.
  No tire, steering, input, suspension, camera, renderer or route authority changes.

6. Explicit limits and negative stress evidence

The retained all-nine-profile ENG4 probes pass at 25 m/s, steering=0.25, full
throttle, G=S=2 or 3, for one second. This is their certified test envelope.
G3/S3 remains test-only; the unchanged browser S selector ends at 2.00.

A separate exploratory 162-case local sweep used all nine profiles, G=S=2 or 3,
ENG=1/3/4, initial speed=0/10/45 m/s and three seconds of full DIRECT throttle,
with steering=1 for the first second then -0.5 for the remaining two seconds.
Existing suspension-travel guards fired in 33 baseline and 47 candidate cases.
These were VehicleOutsideModelError limits, not evidence of a universally valid
high-power maneuver envelope. Removing drive interruption exposes more available
torque; the suspension and its guard were not weakened to hide the limitation.
This release does not certify extreme high-power reversal/crash robustness.

No hands-on browser/phone driving, pleasant-drift or camera-perception success is
claimed. Sustained drift controllability remains a separate DEV_UNCALIBRATED task.
