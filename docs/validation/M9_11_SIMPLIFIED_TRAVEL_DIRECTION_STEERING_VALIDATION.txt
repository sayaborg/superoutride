1. Release scope

M9.11 deliberately simplifies the common steering architecture:

- remove M9.7 yaw-transient steering feedback;
- remove the zero-DC yaw-washout baseline state and time constant;
- delete the retired steering-assist source module rather than preserving a disabled compatibility
  state;
- retain the exact unit-coefficient CG travel-direction geometry;
- retain one physical front road-wheel angle and one existing first-order rack response;
- make vehicle-instance M, D and symmetric actuator response T the only adjustable steering
  calibration values;
- derive automatic authority only as A=M-D; never store A;
- expose D=9/9.5/11/12.5/14 deg, M=37/41/45/49/53 deg and
  T=0.25/0.375/0.5/0.625 s;
- default M=45 deg and T=0.25 s, with profile seed D=9.5 deg for CAR and D=9 deg for BIKE;
- preserve at least A=23 deg for every exposed M x D combination;
- retain M9.10 SLIDE 100/85/80/75/70% as an independent tire calibration, default 100%.

The steering target is:

  A = M - D
  automatic = clamp(betaTravel, -A, +A)
  deltaTarget = clamp(automatic + u*D, -M, +M)

Within automatic authority:

  deltaTarget - betaTravel = u*D

No yaw-rate steering feedback, washout memory, drift mode, drift assist, target sideslip,
travel-direction gain, second rack, tire/steering coupling, vehicle-id branch, drive-layout handling
branch, camera change, route change, topology change or renderer change is introduced.

M9.9's product rule remains: uncontrollable slide is forbidden; controllable drift is allowed.
M9.10's post-peak tire law remains unchanged. Handling remains DEV_UNCALIBRATED and M/D/T remain
future-tunable values rather than a final handling freeze.

5. Executable acceptance recorded by the green run

A. Pure steering geometry
- betaTravel retains structural coefficient exactly 1;
- automatic steering is clamp(betaTravel, -(M-D), +(M-D));
- Driver contribution is exactly u*D;
- final target uses one physical +/-M mechanical clamp;
- within automatic authority, deltaTarget-betaTravel=uD to numerical precision.

B. One authority per steering value
- vehicle-instance calibration owns only M, D and symmetric actuator response;
- A is derived as M-D and is absent as a stored profile/calibration field;
- selector mutation changes calibration without rewriting pose, yaw, velocity or road-wheel state;
- recovery preserves selected calibration while resetting unsafe dynamic state;
- profile reconstruction copies calibration into an independent object rather than sharing mutable
  state.

C. Selector domain
- D choices are exactly 9 / 9.5 / 11 / 12.5 / 14 deg;
- M choices are exactly 37 / 41 / 45 / 49 / 53 deg;
- T choices are exactly 0.25 / 0.375 / 0.5 / 0.625 s;
- Y cycles D, U cycles M and T cycles symmetric actuator traversal;
- default M is 45 deg and default T is 0.25 s;
- CAR profile seed D remains 9.5 deg and BIKE seed D remains 9 deg;
- all 25 exposed M x D pairs derive A=M-D;
- minimum exposed A is exactly 23 deg at M=37, D=14.

D. Catalog-wide physical checks
- all nine production profiles remain finite under the exposed extreme corner
  M=37 deg / D=14 deg / T=0.625 s;
- all nine profiles recover from both -43 deg and +43 deg deep-beta deterministic seeds under the
  explicit recovery input;
- M9.8 product rival regressions on mountain, Tsukuba and FISCO remain green;
- normal route, recovery, camera, renderer and source-boundary integration remains green.

E. Retired authority is absent
- src/physics/steering-assist.ts is absent;
- yawRateBaseline is absent from current steering mechanics;
- yawTransient/yawWashout steering calibration is absent;
- no travelDirectionGain, driftMode or driftAssist steering authority is added;
- no compatibility re-export or disabled replacement state is introduced.

- no M9.11 steering selector changes tire calibration or tire constitutive behavior.
