LIMITS / NOT CERTIFIED

- This is torque protection, NOT ESC. Abrupt steering reversal and braking can still produce lateral spin. No yaw/beta controller was authorized.
- Power-over drift is removed as a product goal; inertial entry/correction/exit remain evaluation goals, not a new completed human-handling certificate.
- The slip envelope uses current pure-X capacity onset as an explicit initial control choice, not a universally optimal combined-slip target. Full diagnostic tire-grid protection calibration is not complete.
- The support barrier is a local frozen-tangent approximation. The bounded search returns a verified feasible candidate, not a proof of global monotonicity, optimum performance or arbitrary-terrain viability.
- Already-excessive slip and inherited pitch momentum may be unreachable through torque reduction alone. Zero-load free wheels, natural crest airborne/recontact, qTravel errors and ordinary overturned recovery remain physical boundaries.
- Full-domain speed/terrain/vehicle behavior and human keyboard/touch feel are not certified by the finite test set.
