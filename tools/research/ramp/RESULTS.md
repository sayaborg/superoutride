# Ramp experiment results (2026-10-26)

Experiment only; never merged. Produced by `tools/research/ramp/ramp-experiment.ts` on a straight 2 km road
(asphalt, unit grip) whose profile is the product `Profile` with one 1 m ramp: its foot is a PVI (kink or vertical
curve of the given length, centred on the foot), its top a sharp kink, its down side 3 m. Each vehicle is spawned on
its static ride 25 m before the ramp at the entry speed and driven by the product mechanics at the fixed step.
Before the ramp a PI speed hold drives an analog throttle; 2 m before the ramp the opening is frozen (the rider
holds it) through the air unless an air input is named. `full`: digital full throttle, rate-limited, throughout.

Columns: pitch is the body's, positive nose up, relative to level; rates °/s positive nose up. Take-off is the first
step with neither contact loaded; landing the first loaded step (or the CG at its ride height above the road).
`air rot` = landing pitch − take-off pitch. `ramp q max` the largest compression on the ramp, `edge q` each
contact's compression at its last loaded step; qTravel 0.30 m, qStatic ≈ 0.11 m. Bump stop: q within 3 mm of qTravel
at a step's end. `peak rate on ramp` the largest rate before the front axle reaches the edge; `rate front at edge`
the rate when it does; `edge Δ` = take-off rate − that; `−g/v` at the take-off speed. `apex` the CG's greatest
height above its ride height on level road. `reaction` the pitch-rate change the wheels' spin changes in the air put
on the body, (I_f Δω_f + I_r Δω_r)/I_pitch. Result: BACK FLIP when pitch passed +90° in the air, overturned when
body up pointed down within 2.5 s after landing.
