Reproduced root cause
---------------------
1. The deployed BRANCHING HUD itself still hardcoded `M6.47 BUILD` even though index.html and package metadata were M6.51.

2. The public fixture retained one deterministic DEV rival starting at parent s=95 and planning RIGHT-B.

3. The first physical route gate was authored at s=545. At the rival's forward plane crossing, the measured stage-local lateral coordinate was approximately +3.32 m.

4. The RIGHT child physical gate is centered near +7.5 m with half-width 3.5 m, so its accepted lateral interval is approximately +4.0..+11.0 m. The rival therefore looked visually committed to RIGHT but physically missed route arbitration by about 0.68 m. RouteDag correctly remained on STAGE_1.

5. The rival then continued along the ordinary open parent Guide. Near parent s≈656.4, the old 100 m curvature lookahead attempted to sample beyond the parent Guide endpoint (L≈766.054 m), throwing RangeError. That unhandled exception terminated the browser animation loop, producing the observed apparent freeze.

Hotfix design
-------------
- Keep the public one-rival fixture. Do not hide the defect by removing the rival.
- Keep FIRST_PHYSICAL_CROSSING_LOCKS and physical crossing as route authority. AI intent never grants route progress.
- Move the first physical route gate from s=545 m to s=570 m. This remains after full road separation and before the existing s=600 m handoff seam, preserving a distinct physical gate -> PENDING -> physical seam -> COMMIT interval.
- At s=570 the actual Pages rival crosses the RIGHT gate physically inside the child road (measured local l≈+7.90 m in the continuous 60 Hz regression).
- Bound deterministic rival steering and curvature lookahead to the real finite open Guide endpoint. This is endpoint clipping, not cyclic wrapping or topology inference.
- Correct the BRANCHING HUD milestone to M6.51.
- Add a package-derived visible milestone synchronization regression covering index.html, BRANCHING HUD and CIRCUIT HUD.

Architecture preserved
----------------------
- World X/Y/Z remains vehicle physics authority.
- Route choice remains physical gate crossing only.
- Route gate -> PENDING -> physical seam -> COMMIT remains unchanged.
- Renderer/Core receives no route or topology special case.
- Open Guide remains open; no modulo or wrap is introduced.
- Public rival cardinality remains 1 and the product 0..16 rival architecture remains intact.
- Fixed presentation authority remains 2.0 m = 80 px.

Direct incident regressions
---------------------------
1. `open Guide rival lookahead never samples beyond the endpoint`
   Verifies steering and speed-lookahead sampling remain finite near the real open Guide end.

2. `actual Pages rival physically takes RIGHT first fork, commits child runtime and keeps driving`
   Uses the real DEV rival start s=95, real RIGHT-B route plan, ordinary M5 car physics and recovery at 60 Hz. It requires:
   - physical S1_RIGHT acceptance inside the RIGHT child-road width,
   - seam COMMIT to CONTENT_STAGE_2_R,
   - continued child-stage driving to at least child s=150 m.

3. `visible Pages milestone labels match the package milestone`
   Prevents a future deployed build from showing an old milestone label while the package is newer.
